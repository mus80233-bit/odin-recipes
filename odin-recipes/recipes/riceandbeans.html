Rice and Beans

Rice and Beans is a classic dish enjoyed in many cultures around the world. It is a simple yet flavorful combination of rice and beans, often seasoned with herbs and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of long-grain rice
  • 1 cup of black beans, cooked
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
