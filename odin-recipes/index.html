Odin Recipes

Eba and Egusi

This is a traditional Nigerian dish made with Eba (a starchy side made from cassava flour) and Egusi soup (a thick soup made from melon seeds, vegetables, and meat or fish).

Jollof Rice

Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions, and a variety of spices. It is often served with fried plantains, grilled chicken, or fish.

Rice and Beans

Rice and Beans is a classic dish enjoyed in many cultures around the world. It is a simple yet flavorful combination of rice and beans, often seasoned with herbs and spices.
