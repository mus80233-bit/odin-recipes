Jollof Rice

Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, onions, and a variety of spices. It is often served with fried plantains, grilled chicken, or fish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of long-grain rice
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
